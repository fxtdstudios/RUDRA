We train a compact (1.2M-parameter) network for single-image SDR-to-HDR reconstruction and evaluate it against its own analytic inverse tone map on 429 held-out frames in PU21-PSNR and ColorVideoVDP JOD. On degraded input the model gains +1.43 dB and +0.44 JOD, winning 81% of frames. On clean, well-graded input it loses 3.0 dB of PU21-PSNR while CVVDP puts the same gap at -0.046 JOD: a two-order-of-magnitude disagreement between the metrics that is itself a result.

The clean regression concentrates in low-dynamic-range frames. The 60 worst average -10.76 dB at a median ground-truth peak of 238 nits; the 60 best +3.41 dB at 19,590 nits. The model has no representation of how much headroom a frame has, and so cannot decide to do nothing.

We measure what fixing that is worth and whether it can be fixed. An oracle per-frame scale on the residual is worth +5.84 dB on clean and +0.29 dB on degraded simultaneously, and no constant achieves it. But a linear readout of the features such a gate would use explains only 3% of that scale's variance, and 79% of the variance is within a condition rather than between, so even a perfect clean-versus-degraded classifier caps at R^2 0.213.

That bound is a property of the question, not the problem: posed as a continuous scale it is unreachable, posed as a binary decision on the detectable axis it is reachable. A 21,121-parameter gate on the shadow arm moves clean input by +3.41 +/- 0.32 dB and +0.135 +/- 0.023 JOD over the shipped model for 0.30 +/- 0.15 dB and 0.091 +/- 0.041 JOD on degraded input, over three seeds, all positive on all four measures against the analytic baseline.

Against ExpandNet on the same 429 frames, run from the authors' released weights, RUDRA is +18.56 dB and +2.03 JOD. Both metrics agree there, so the disagreement above is a property of small differences, not of the instruments.
