Close the comparison gap: ExpandNet on the 429-frame split

RUDRA + gate scores 46.06 dB / 9.561 JOD against ExpandNet's 27.50 / 7.532,
run from the authors' released weights with their own pre- and postprocessing.
-18.56 dB and -2.029 JOD, winning 1 of 429 frames on PU21 and 16 on CVVDP.

Both metrics agree here, which settles something about section 5: the
two-order-of-magnitude disagreement is a property of small differences on
well-graded input, not a defect in either instrument.

Three caveats in 5.1 rather than in a reviewer's report -- training-domain
mismatch, an unclamped reference, and a per-frame exposure fit spanning 35.5x
for ExpandNet against 1.0x for RUDRA. Given the best possible exposure it
recovers +0.39 dB, so the fit is not what costs it. Put through the same fit
RUDRA gains 0.55 dB and loses 0.0025 JOD, so the row we report for ourselves
is RUDRA as deployed.

Section 9's "largest gap" becomes "one published method, not four".
